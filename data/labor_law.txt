Karensavdrag Rules and Calculation
Definition:
The karensavdrag is a one-time deduction equal to 20% of the employee's average weekly sick pay. Sick pay (sjuklön) is 80% of the employee's salary and benefits.

Calculation Steps:

Determine hourly sick pay: Hourly salary * 0.8.

Determine average weekly sick pay: Average weekly working hours * Hourly sick pay.

Calculate deduction: Average weekly sick pay * 0.2.

Examples:

Regular (40h/week, 110kr/h): Sick pay = 88kr/h. Weekly = 3,520kr. Deduction = 704kr.

Part-time (32h/week, 150kr/h): Sick pay = 120kr/h. Weekly = 3,840kr. Deduction = 768kr.

Irregular (30h/week, 110kr/h): Sick pay = 88kr/h. Weekly = 2,640kr. Deduction = 528kr.

Key Regulations:

The deduction amount is the same regardless of when the employee becomes ill.

If an employee becomes ill mid-day, the deduction applies proportionally until the full karensavdrag amount is reached.

Limited companies (aktiebolag): Owners are treated as employees.

Sole traders (enskild firma/handelsbolag): Not affected by these rules.
