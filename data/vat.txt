VAT GUIDELINES:

[VAT RATES]:
- Standard: 25%
- Reduced: 12% and 6%
- VAT-exempt: Some sales are exempt (check specific criteria).

[REGISTRATION]:
- Mandatory registration needed for most businesses.
- Exemption: Possible if annual turnover is <= 120,000 SEK.

[EU SALES & OSS]:
- B2C (EU): Use One Stop Shop (OSS) e-service for reporting and paying VAT in the customer's country.
- VAT-exempt sales: Possible for certain EU sales if requirements are met (e.g., SME scheme).

[DEDUCTIONS]:
- VAT deductions allowed for business-related entertainment expenses (negotiations/staff welfare).

[SOURCES]:
- Skatteverket (Official Swedish Tax Agency)
